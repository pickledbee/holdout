Hall of Fame

How to play

  • Answer one question a day. Your first one is ready right now.
  • Everyone's next question unlocks at midnight Central Time.
  • Questions get harder as you go. Get 30 in a row right to make the Hall of Fame.
  • One wrong answer ends your run.
  • Busy day? You get 2 pause days per run. Missing a day uses one, and your question waits for you.
  • Knocked out? Start a new run for free, or invite a friend: when they join with your link, you can continue from where you left off.
  • Each answer is revealed after the next midnight, so no spoilers.
  • Think a question was unfair? Report it. If it was wrong, everyone it knocked out gets brought back.
Privacy and rules

Players must be 13 or older.

We only store the display name you choose, a scrambled version of your PIN, when you joined, your answers and how long they took, and a random code for your browser that helps us spot cheating. We do not ask for your email or real name, and we never sell or share your information.

Playing more than one account, or making fake accounts to earn comebacks, gets you removed.

Your display name appears on the public leaderboard, so do not use your full real name.

There are no prizes. Bragging rights only.

To have your player deleted, contact the game organizer.

Product picks link to Amazon. Clicking or buying never affects your game.
