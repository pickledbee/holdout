Hall of Fame

How to play

  • Your game starts the moment you join. Your Day 1 question is waiting right away.
  • A new question unlocks every 24 hours from the time you joined.
  • You have 24 hours to answer each one.
  • One wrong answer, or a skipped day, and you are out.
  • Survive every day to make the Hall of Fame.
  • Invite friends with your personal link. Every friend who joins earns you a bonus question (up to 2 a day).
  • Still in? A bonus lets you answer your next question right now instead of waiting.
  • Knocked out? A bonus puts you back in right where you left off.
Privacy and rules

Players must be 13 or older.

We only store the display name you choose, a scrambled version of your PIN, when you joined, your answers and how long they took, and a random code for your browser that helps us spot cheating. We do not ask for your email or real name, and we never sell or share your information.

Playing more than one account, or making fake accounts to earn bonus questions, gets you removed.

Your display name appears on the public leaderboard, so do not use your full real name.

There are no prizes. Bragging rights only.

To have your player deleted, contact the game organizer.

Product picks link to Amazon. Clicking or buying never affects your game.
