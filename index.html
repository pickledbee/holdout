Holdout

How many trivia questions can you get right in a row?
Free · no sign-up · ages 13+

How to play

  • Play: answer as many questions as you like. Every right answer adds 1 to your streak. One wrong answer ends the run, then just start another. Questions get harder the longer you last.
  • See the answer and a fun fact right after every question.
  • Daily 10: the same 10 questions for everyone, once a day. One miss ends it. Answers are shown the next day, so share your squares, not the answers.
  • Reach 30 in a row to get into the Hall of Fame.
  • Comebacks: when a friend uses your link and answers 3 questions, you get a comeback. Use it to keep a run going after a wrong answer (up to 2 a day).
  • No sign-up needed. You get a fun name to start. Pick your own name and a PIN any time to save your spot and play on other devices.
  • Think a question is wrong? Report it right from the answer screen.
Privacy and rules

Players must be 13 or older. By playing, you confirm you are.

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of the game people use. It uses cookies and does not receive your display name or PIN.

We only store your display name (an automatic fun name until you pick one), a scrambled version of your PIN if you set one, your answers and how long they took, and a random code for your browser that helps us spot cheating. We do not ask for your email or real name, and we never sell or share your information.

Playing more than one account, or making fake accounts to earn comebacks, gets you removed.

Your display name appears on the public leaderboard, so do not use your full real name.

There are no prizes. Bragging rights only.

To have your player deleted, contact the game organizer.

Product picks link to Amazon. Clicking or buying never affects your game.
